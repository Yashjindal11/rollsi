ROLLSI

Analyst charts, think-cell-style annotations and rolling-stat functions for Excel.

Select your data (header row + columns) and click a chart. Charts stay live: they read formulas on the ROLLSI Data sheet.

Click a column or line chart, then add think-cell-style annotations. Category numbers count from 1 (left to right).

Difference arrow

Annotations are shapes drawn on the sheet. After you resize a chart or change its data, click Refresh all.

Palette

Consultant style

No gridlines or borders, muted axes, K/M number formats, palette colours, legend only when needed.

Slide sizes drop straight into a 16:9 PowerPoint slide. If a download is blocked, use Copy or drag the image into PowerPoint.
